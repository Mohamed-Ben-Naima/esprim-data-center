# Site and buildings

![Site plan](img/plan_masse.png)

Coordinates are in metres, in the Blender world: X points east, Y points north, and the origin is near the
centre of the campus.

## Campus

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Fenced site | 276 × 186 m (fence at x = ±138, y = ±93) |
| Public road | South edge, y = −104 |
| Entrance | Single controlled gate at x ≈ −60 on the south fence |
| Internal ring road | 9 m wide, around the three buildings |
| Parking | 60 × 40 m, about 80 spaces, two rows of solar carports, 4 EV chargers |
| Landscaping | Irrigated lawn and tree line inside the fence, dry natural land outside |

## Bâtiment informatique (IT building)

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Footprint | 70 × 40 m, 11 m high, centre (5, 20) |
| Envelope | Windowless graphite cladding (security, no solar gain), aluminium fins, cyan LED lines |
| Inside (phase 3) | White room of about 1,500 m², about 200 racks in hot/cold aisles, meet-me room, storage |
| Roof | 21 dry coolers in 3 rows (heat rejection and free cooling) and chilled-water pipes |
| North side | 4 loading-dock doors (equipment deliveries) |
| Links | Covered bridge east to the technical building, glass corridor south-west to the logistique building |

## Bâtiment technique (technical building)

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Footprint | 36 × 30 m, 10 m high, centre (73, 27) |
| Envelope | Ribbed steel with large ventilation louvres |
| Inside (phase 3) | TGBT A and B, UPS A and B with battery rooms, chilled-water pumps |
| Roof | 4 chillers (3 + 1) |
| Outdoor | 6 generators G1–G6 (N+1) in a row south of the building, 2 fuel tanks in a retention bund |

## Energy yard

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Substation | Gravel yard 26 × 56 m on the east edge |
| Transformers | TR-A (118, 30) and TR-B (118, 2), 2.5 MVA each |
| HV gantries | One per feed, 14 m high |
| HV lines | Feed A heads north-east, feed B heads east-south-east. Four 43 m lattice pylons per line with aviation beacons |
